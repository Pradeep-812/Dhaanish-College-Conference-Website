About The Conference

Bringing together researchers, academicians, industry experts, and students to discuss emerging technologies.

Our Institution

Dhaanish Chennai College of Engineering is committed to academic excellence, technological advancement, research innovation and holistic student development. The institution strives to create a dynamic learning environment that encourages interdisciplinary research, industry collaboration and the development of future-ready professionals.

ICISIIME 2027

The International Conference on Intelligent Systems, Industrial Innovation and Management Excellence aims to provide a premier platform to discuss emerging technologies, innovative engineering solutions and contemporary management practices. Join us to bridge the gap between intelligent tech, engineering, and transformative management.
